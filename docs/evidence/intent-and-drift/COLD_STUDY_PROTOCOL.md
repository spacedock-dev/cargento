# Observer protocol for the cold usability walks

Give this file to the observer agent or human facilitator without showing it to the participant. The participant sees only [the kickoff and consent note](PARTICIPANT_KICKOFF.md) before the task sentence. If they read this protocol, the [sidecar prompt](PROMPT.md), or another control walkthrough, their run is exploratory rather than one of [DRC-4722](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4722)'s two cold walks.

1. Check readiness before recruiting either participant. DRC-4717 and DRC-4683 must be Done, their verdicts must apply to the target build, and any fixes must be merged to `main`. Record the full `origin/main` SHA. If these conditions are not met, use the exploratory sidecar prompt with other volunteers and reserve the two cold participants. A panel change after a cold walk may require a new participant for the fixed step, as DRC-4722 says.
2. Obtain the participant's agreement to the kickoff note. Starting Cargento scans available local session stores across supported harnesses; the board may display other titles and prompts. The observer may shortlist metadata for the three freshest Claude Code sessions in the participant's project and read only the one they select. The coding agent's provider may receive what it reads. The participant decides separately whether to allow Cargento's model reading on its browser disclosure. Do not let the agent press Allow.
3. Prove observation works before the participant sees the task or any Cargento controls. On a neutral Chrome tab in the participant's actual window, ask them to make one ordinary click; the observer must see it, time and count it, and save a sample screenshot with measured viewport width. Let the participant inspect this non-sensitive sample. If the agent cannot observe and capture this, arrange a human observer, approved by the participant, who can take the notes and screenshots. Without one of those arrangements, stop the qualifying walk and mark any later trial preparatory. Do not consume a cold participant on an unobservable run.
4. From a separate checkout of current `origin/main`, start the documented Cargento launcher on an unused loopback port with a new private `CARGENTO_HOME`. Record the SHA and flags. Leave `--observer-model` off and do not use `--no-observer-model` for this Analyze walk. Do not stop or reuse another dashboard. Shortlist the three freshest real Claude Code sessions in this project. Let the participant choose one; note why a newer one was skipped. Use the documented all-sessions view (`?all=1`) if it is outside the default window. Do not alter its transcript or stage work to make it drift.
5. Before the task, confirm that the chosen row has a usable reading route and the named CLI/provider is available, without pressing Analyze or showing the participant a result. The Claude producer gate may still be closed, so a Codex fallback may be needed. If there is no usable route, do not start a qualifying run. Record the disabled reason as preparatory evidence and reserve the participant for a later build.
6. Put Chrome on the Sessions view with the chosen row visible. Let the participant drive from that row. Give exactly this sentence and no control hints: "Find out whether this session is still doing what you asked, and if it is not, get it back on track." Observe silently. Record the presses and elapsed time from the row to a result and to a copied correction, whether the draft was checked, each hesitation and its screen, whether a cited entry was found, what "Can't tell" meant to the participant, the level's source, the Steer back or Update intent choice, and whether a pasted correction helped. The participant makes any first-press consent decision in Chrome. If they decline, do not work around it; the walk cannot meet the result criterion.
7. After the walk, ask, without supplying answers: "What did the level tell you?", "Would you leave the live monitor on?", and "What would you have done without Cargento?" Record their answers in their own words and ask what they approve for a public summary and for the Linear issue attachment. Do not paraphrase an answer and then call it verbatim.
8. Save each screenshot under `docs/screenshots/DRC-4722/` in the retained checkout, named by step and actual width. A person reads every image before a pass or fail verdict. Give the owner a participant-approved packet with a table of step, run label, width, pass/fail/partial, screenshot, and cited entry numbers or approved UI strings. The owner combines both participants in one DRC-4722 verdict comment and attaches each participant-approved, reviewed screenshot and the approved session notes and verbatim answers. If any required evidence is withheld or cannot be attached, keep the local copy and mark the verdict incomplete or `pending owner upload`; a public Git summary alone is not acceptance evidence.
9. Apply DRC-4722's thresholds. More than three presses to a result or four to a copied correction is a failure and a defect in this milestone. So is a participant misunderstanding where the level came from or what "Can't tell" means. List every misread string with the design or overrule that governs it so the owner can decide whether the ruling should change. Draft a redacted defect issue with the observed wording, SHA, and reproduction, and get participant approval of its exact content and destination before filing it. If approval is withheld, keep the finding locally and mark the verdict incomplete; do not silently turn it into a pass. Link an approved defect from the verdict comment. A fixed step needs a new cold participant unless the owner accepts the fix in a sentence on the issue.
10. Fill [the run template](RUN_TEMPLATE.md) with a redacted public summary only after participant review. Commit it on a branch and open a PR into `main`; never commit raw sessions, screenshots, clipboard text, paths, IDs, or unapproved answers. Stop only the sidecar server you started. Keep the private checkout, screenshots, and scratch home until the participant confirms the issue handoff and approves cleanup.

The two completed participant walks, not the agent's own interaction with the page, settle DRC-4722. Keep its `blockedBy` relations and status until the required verdicts are accepted.
