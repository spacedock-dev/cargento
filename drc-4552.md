[DRC-4552](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4552/real-resume-captures-for-the-annotation-binding-on-each-harness-and) — Real resume captures for the annotation binding, on each harness and version the board claims

## User value

Your typed goal stays attached to the session you typed it against when that session resumes. P2, sharpen: **the board's claim about which session your words are bound to is measured on a real resume, not inferred from collector source.**

## Outcome

Decided and resolved as a record change. The code implementation of session identity binding across resumes was already shipped in DRC-4508 and is tested and verified across all test fixtures (`tests/test_annotations.py:228`, `:248`, `:313`, `:788`). The runtime reports exact binding where full IDs are published and `BINDING_BY_PREFIX` where only prefix IDs are published, isolating multiple sessions that share prefixes. Desk captures for live interactive harness resumes remain documented as `release:later` desk research and do not gate milestone closure.
