### Captured original — DRC-4610 body (verbatim)

````markdown
## User value

No user sees this change and the move is `keep`. It matters because a criterion aimed at dead code certifies nothing: the test passes, the gate reads it as evidence, and the surface the criterion was written to protect is not on screen at all.

## The problem

`nextProjectDelegation` at `next-delegation.js:168-186` is the function <issue id="53b75c53-66ba-4dce-a929-2dc0e89a453e" href="https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4589/split-the-three-inks-onto-label-value-and-absence-roles-so-a-label">DRC-4589</issue>'s AC-2 names as the Console "no figure yet" path. It **has no caller anywhere in the runtime**, and has not since `8d2585c`.

It is stamped and unit-tested, but only by direct call. So the test is green, the criterion reads as satisfied, and nothing establishes that a reader ever meets the markup it renders. <issue id="53b75c53-66ba-4dce-a929-2dc0e89a453e" href="https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4589/split-the-three-inks-onto-label-value-and-absence-roles-so-a-label">DRC-4589</issue>'s implementation found this while verifying its own criterion rather than while reading the file, said so in the test's docstring so the test does not read as covering a rendered path, and filed it rather than deleting anything.

Two distinct things are wrong and they need separating before either is acted on:

* **The code may be dead.** If nothing calls it and nothing should, it goes, along with its test and its stamp.
* **The criterion may be aimed at the wrong place.** If the Console genuinely renders a "no figure yet" absence, it does so by some other path, and <issue id="53b75c53-66ba-4dce-a929-2dc0e89a453e" href="https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4589/split-the-three-inks-onto-label-value-and-absence-roles-so-a-label">DRC-4589</issue>'s AC-2 was verified against a function that is not the one a reader sees. That is the more serious half, and it is invisible while the test is green.

This is the same family as the guards this milestone has already found: a check that passes while the claim it stands for is untested. Five of those have now surfaced here, and every one was green and mutation-checked.

## The solution

Establish which of the two it is, in this order, because the answer changes what ships.

1. Find what actually renders the Console's absent-figure state today. If it is another function, <issue id="53b75c53-66ba-4dce-a929-2dc0e89a453e" href="https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4589/split-the-three-inks-onto-label-value-and-absence-roles-so-a-label">DRC-4589</issue>'s AC-2 was verified against the wrong target and the record needs correcting — say so plainly rather than quietly repointing it.
2. Then, and only then, decide the code. Remove it with its test and stamp if nothing renders it; wire it back if the Console lost a path it should have.

The rejected alternative is deleting it now on the strength of the caller count. That closes the cheap half and permanently hides the expensive one: a criterion verified against an unrendered path leaves no trace once the path is gone.

## Acceptance

* **AC-1 — offline:** The function that renders the Console's absent-figure state is named, and it is reachable from a caller in the runtime. **Verified by:** a call-graph trace from the Console render entry point to the emitting function, recorded in the issue. **Falsified by:** naming a function whose only caller is a test.
* **AC-2 — offline:** Either `nextProjectDelegation` and its test and stamp are gone, or it has a runtime caller. **Verified by:** `grep -rn "nextProjectDelegation" cargento/skills/cargento/cargento_runtime/` returning either nothing or a call site outside its own definition. **Falsified by:** the definition surviving with test-only references.
* **AC-3 — offline:** If AC-1 finds a different emitter, <issue id="53b75c53-66ba-4dce-a929-2dc0e89a453e" href="https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4589/split-the-three-inks-onto-label-value-and-absence-roles-so-a-label">DRC-4589</issue>'s AC-2 record carries a dated correction naming what it was actually verified against. **Verified by:** the correction present on that issue. **Falsified by:** the criterion silently repointed with no record that it had been verified elsewhere.

## Provenance

Filed 2026-09-17 from <issue id="53b75c53-66ba-4dce-a929-2dc0e89a453e" href="https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4589/split-the-three-inks-onto-label-value-and-absence-roles-so-a-label">DRC-4589</issue>'s implementation, which found it while verifying its own acceptance criterion rather than while reading the file, and reported it instead of quietly satisfying the criterion by direct call.
````

### Captured original — milestone `Clean and Cogent UI/UX` description (verbatim)

````markdown
## The user value

**The board can be read at a glance: you can tell a label from its answer, a figure from a gap, and the one thing to do on a screen from the prose around it.**

Users report low contrast. Every text ink in the cockpit passes WCAG AA — ink3, the dimmest, is 5.67:1 on panel, nearer AAA than the AA floor. Measured size-aware with APCA, the same inks fail badly at the sizes they are actually set in: at 12.5px/400 even `#f4f1e8`, the brightest ink in the palette, scores Lc 98.0 against a body-text requirement of 100, and the 9.5px and 10px label steps fall off the bottom of the lookup table entirely.

So there is no colour left to spend and no contrast fix available. The work is to raise the sentence tier, collapse the label tier, and give label, value and absence three distinct registers instead of the one dim ink that currently carries 89% of the characters on Held to.

## What is left

Fifteen issues. Three are foundations and everything else depends on them:

* **Raise board sentences to a 15px tier** and collapse six sub-12px tokens into one label tier.
* **Split the three inks onto label, value and absence roles**, so a label stops sharing its ink with its own answer.
* **Always render the Held to reading control**, present and inert with its reason, instead of deleting the tab's only verb exactly when a newcomer looks for it.

Then: the briefing, the tab strip, a control primitive with a primary action on the one tab that has one, caveat tiering, Held to ordering, Console ordering, the scope rail, the timeline filter, and a guardrail so the asset test enforces size rather than the contrast that already passes. Two issues were split out of the primitive at triage: giving the other four tabs a main action to mark, which is a product question rather than a styling one, and the ten control recipes the primitive's enumerated set does not reach.

## Waits on

Nothing outside this milestone. `type-scale` and `ink-roles` gate most of the rest; `ink-roles` itself waits on `type-scale`.

## How this was measured

Contrast computed from the shipped tokens and independently recomputed during triage, matching to the decimal. APCA from the same pairs at the sizes they render at — **audit-only: no APCA implementation, table or fixture exists in the repository, so these figures cannot be reproduced from the tree.** No issue in this milestone adds an APCA implementation, so these figures stay audit-only. [DRC-4596](<https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596/make-the-asset-test-enforce-font-size-not-the-contrast-that-already>) guards the **px floors** the audit motivated — the 11px label tier and the 15px sentence tier — which is a different and weaker property than reproducing the audit. It cannot see a sentence whose size, family and line-height are composed across several rules; two such sentences remain and [DRC-4602](<https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4602/sans-rules-still-sit-below-the-15px-sentence-floor-and-a-per-rule>) sizes them. Density, ink distribution and control counts read off the live DOM at `127.0.0.1:4553`. Density figures are floors — they were taken on a quiet, unannotated session, and a busy project has more text, not less. Control counts are floors in the same way and are **data-dependent**: retaken on a three-session project during [DRC-4595](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4595/put-consoles-operations-first-and-lift-the-steer-box-out-of-tripwires)'s triage, Console's 5 reproduced but Now and Course came back 3 and 3 against the 0 and 1 on file. They illustrate the imbalance; no acceptance criterion in this milestone may rest on one.

Eleven claims were raised and rejected rather than filed. The two worth knowing: brightening the inks cannot fix C1, and merging the three thin tabs is a route change plus an amendment to NUI-3, not a layout change.
````
