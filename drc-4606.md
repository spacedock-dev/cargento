[DRC-4606](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4606/six-claims-in-the-design-doc-have-nothing-binding-them-and-the-worst) — Six claims in the design doc have nothing binding them

Filed 2026-09-17 from DRC-4587 cycle-4 review, which audited each claim in docs/design-next-ui.md
for a binding rather than assuming one. Six are prose only. The worst is the tracking census:
Copilot found it wrong once, it was corrected in prose, then expanded in prose, and zero tests
mention letter-spacing. Added to this milestone on the captain standing rule that a gap deferred
out of a PR is worked inside the same milestone rather than filed and forgotten.

## Adversarial read

Checked against merged `main` at `84d27a5` (PR #361), not against the issue's own prose. A snapshot
of that tree was taken with `git archive origin/main` because the working checkout is one merge
behind; every figure below was measured in that snapshot.

**The problem is still real, and larger than filed.** The issue says items 2 and 3 were "verified by
hand in one review cycle, unpinned since". Measured on the merged tree they do not reproduce at all:

| Claim in `docs/design-next-ui.md` | What the doc says | Measured on `84d27a5` |
|---|---|---|
| sentence-tier population | "all sixty-seven resolve to `var(--fs-sentence)`" | 54 declaration blocks, 70 selectors (69 outside `@media`) |
| `--measure` split | "Forty-nine of them also cap", "The eighteen that do not" | 55 selectors cap (40 blocks); 15 do not (14 blocks) |
| the uncapped reasons | "eight carry `overflow-wrap:anywhere`" | 5 of the 14 uncapped blocks declare it |
| tracking census | 24 at `.09em`, 3 exceptions, 6 further | reproduces **exactly**, including all three named selectors |
| `--fs-label` | "at 11px" | `--fs-label:11px` — true |

Four counting conventions were tried for sixty-seven — declaration blocks, selectors, selectors
outside `@media`, and rules satisfying the doc's own membership test — and none returns 67. The doc
states no convention, which is the defect rather than a detail of it: a figure whose derivation is
unstated cannot be re-derived, and the branch it was taken on was deleted at merge, so its
provenance is gone too.

**This also moves a sibling.** [DRC-4596](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596)'s AC-3 says its
census "classifies 86 rules, 67 at 15px and 19 below, reproducing the doc's own figures". The same
classification over merged `main` returns 87 rules, 54 at 15px and 33 below. That criterion's cited
baseline is stale before implementation starts; it is recorded here for that issue's gate rather
than edited from here.

**The reversal to note:** item 1 has the worst *record* and zero coverage but is correct today;
items 2 and 3 are wrong **now**. The filed order (tracking census first) is kept — it is the
captain's standing order and the issue's own ruling, and all four stylesheet claims are bound by
one test module over one file, so the order decides which criterion is written first rather than
what ships.

**Nothing in the body describes a state that no longer exists**, with one exception: item 6's bound
table lives in the archived entity `_archive/drc-4587/index.md`, not in `docs/design-next-ui.md`, so
the issue title's "in the design doc" is true of five of the six.

### The six, and where each is bound

1. **Tracking census** — **bound here**, first. Fully derivable from the stylesheet: all 45
   `letter-spacing` declarations partition into the `.09em` tier plus an enumerated register.
2. **`--measure` census** — **bound here**. Same shape, same file, and wrong today.
3. **"all sixty-seven resolve to `var(--fs-sentence)`"** — **bound here as the declared-tier
   property**, with the population figure moved out of prose and into the test that derives it. The
   *resolved* census — what an element renders at after the cascade — stays
   [DRC-4596](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596)'s AC-3 and
   [DRC-4602](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4602)'s composed audit. One number, one owner: this
   issue owns which rules *declare* the tier, they own what *resolves* to it.
4. **"`--fs-label` at 11px"** — **split**. The `:root` floor over every token is
   [DRC-4596](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596)'s AC-1 and is not re-bound here. Bound here
   is the residue nothing else covers: the token's own value, the three sites that write 11px as a
   literal instead of the token, and the eight `--fs-*` tokens that have no caller at all — the
   property that keeps "collapsed into one tier" true rather than the property that a token is not
   retuned downward.
5. **The membership test** — **handed to [DRC-4602](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4602)**,
   unchanged from the filed reasoning. Its AC-1 and AC-2 build exactly the composed-cascade audit
   that a membership test enumerating elements would be, and a second implementation here would be
   the same number with two owners.
6. **The bound table** — **bound here**, as a derived capacity register over `web/*.js`. The
   helper-call half already re-derives exactly; the file-bucket half does not, and F9 named three
   files it puts on the wrong side.

## Approach, and the simplest rejected alternative

**Chosen:** one test module that resolves each set from the source it is a claim about and asserts a
partition over it, with every exception an explicit register entry carrying its reason. The
promotion test on DRC-4587 is the model, and `tests/css_cascade.py` already supplies the restricted
resolver, so the new work is declaration scanning rather than cascade work.

**Rejected — the documentation lint.** Grep the prose for stale figures. It is simpler and it cannot
deliver: across four sweeps of this document one retracted count survived as digits, then
number-words, then a comparison, then a hedge, so the search is never finished. It also binds the
spelling of a claim rather than the property, which means it passes on a document that is wrong and
fails on one that is right and reworded.

**Rejected — assert the figures the document already carries.** Simpler again, and it would have
shipped two false assertions today: 67 and 49 are not what the tree returns. The test derives the
number and the document stops carrying one.
