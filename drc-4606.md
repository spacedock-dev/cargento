[DRC-4606](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4606/six-claims-in-the-design-doc-have-nothing-binding-them-and-the-worst) — Six claims in the design doc have nothing binding them

Filed 2026-09-17 from DRC-4587 cycle-4 review, which audited each claim in docs/design-next-ui.md
for a binding rather than assuming one. Six are prose only. The worst is the tracking census:
Copilot found it wrong once, it was corrected in prose, then expanded in prose, and zero tests
mention letter-spacing. Added to this milestone on the captain standing rule that a gap deferred
out of a PR is worked inside the same milestone rather than filed and forgotten.

## Adversarial read

**The pinned SHA was stale, and the claim moved underneath it. Every figure below is re-derived at
`21a0e93`, the merged `origin/main` tip.** This triage was dispatched to derive against
`d9059ae3f0e2f73f40977bf5315afea994a56b3e`, and that SHA is recorded as instructed. It is no longer
the tip: PR #362 (`spacedock-ensign/drc-4588`) merged after it as `21a0e93`, changing `styles.css`
by 72 lines, `next-cockpit.js` by 158 and `docs/design-next-ui.md` by 60 — the stylesheet, the
emitter and the prose that all six claims are about. `d9059ae` itself differs from the prior read's
`84d27a5` only in `docs/roadmap-burndown/README.md`, so the three trees form one chain and the
figures are reported against the tip.

Nothing was carried from the issue prose, from the prior read, or from the archived DRC-4587 entity;
each figure below was produced by a brace-walking parser over the tip snapshot, and the two places
where an independent derivation disagreed with the prior read are recorded as such.

**This is the issue's own thesis, demonstrated during its own triage.** One merged PR moved the
sentence census within hours of the read that pinned it, and nothing in the suite noticed — because,
as verified at the tip, **no test file mentions `letter-spacing` and none mentions `fs-label`**
(`grep -rl` over `tests/`, empty both times).

| Claim in `docs/design-next-ui.md` | What the doc says | Measured at `21a0e93` |
|---|---|---|
| sentence-tier population | "all sixty-seven resolve to `var(--fs-sentence)`" | 55 declaration blocks, 71 selectors (54 blocks / 70 selectors outside `@media`) |
| `--measure` split | "Forty-nine of them also cap", "The eighteen that do not" | 55 selectors cap (40 blocks); 16 do not (15 blocks) |
| the uncapped reasons | "eight carry `overflow-wrap:anywhere`" | 5 of the 15 uncapped blocks declare it |
| tracking census | 24 at `.09em`, 3 exceptions, 6 further | reproduces **exactly**, and partitions all 45 declarations |
| `--fs-label` | "at 11px" | `--fs-label:11px` — true |

**What PR #362 moved, measured across the pair:** the sentence tier went from 54 blocks/70 selectors
to 55/71 and the uncapped set from 14 blocks to 15, because `.next-action` arrived declaring the
tier without a `--measure` cap. The capped set, the `overflow-wrap` count, the whole tracking
partition, both token values and all three 11px literals are unchanged. So one of the five claims
drifted again, in the window between this issue being filed and being triaged.

Four counting conventions were tried for sixty-seven — declaration blocks, selectors, selectors
outside `@media`, and rules satisfying the doc's own membership test — and none returns 67 at either
SHA. The doc states no convention, which is the defect rather than a detail of it: a figure whose
derivation is unstated cannot be re-derived, and the branch it was taken on was deleted at merge, so
its provenance is gone too.

**A shorthand-blind census understates this set by a factor of nearly three, and that was measured
here rather than assumed.** A first pass reading only `font-size` returned 20 blocks, 12 capped and
8 uncapped. The correct figures are 55/40/15, because **34 of the 54 `var(--fs-sentence)` uses sit
in the `font:` shorthand**, not in `font-size`. The design doc warns about exactly this failure and
it still caught a fresh derivation, which is why AC-3 requires a parser that raises on a shorthand
it cannot read rather than one that returns a plausible total.

**This also moves a sibling, and the honest report is weaker than the prior read's.**
[DRC-4596](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596)'s AC-3 says its census "classifies 86 rules, 67
at 15px and 19 below, reproducing the doc's own figures". The prior read of this entity recorded
that the same classification returns "87 rules, 54 at 15px and 33 below" on merged `main`. **That
figure is not re-derived here and should not be carried.** Classifying every block in the tip
stylesheet with a resolvable declared size returns 317 blocks, 73 at 15px or above and 244 below —
which matches neither the criterion's 86/67/19 nor the prior read's 87/54/33, because AC-3 does not
state which rules its census admits and no population I could reconstruct returns a total near 86.

What is safe to hand DRC-4596's gate is therefore the defect class rather than a replacement number:
its AC-3 cites a baseline whose population is unstated, so the criterion cannot be checked by a
fresh agent — the same failure this issue exists to fix, one issue over. It is recorded for that
gate and not edited from here.

**The reversal to note:** item 1 has the worst *record* and zero coverage but is correct today;
items 2 and 3 are wrong **now**. The filed order (tracking census first) is kept — it is the
captain's standing order and the issue's own ruling, and all four stylesheet claims are bound by
one test module over one file, so the order decides which criterion is written first rather than
what ships.

**Nothing in the body describes a state that no longer exists**, with one exception: item 6's bound
table lives in the archived entity `_archive/drc-4587/index.md`, not in `docs/design-next-ui.md`, so
the issue title's "in the design doc" is true of five of the six.

### The six, and where each is bound

1. **Tracking census** — **bound here**, first. Fully derivable from the stylesheet: all 45
   `letter-spacing` declarations partition into the `.09em` tier plus an enumerated register.
2. **`--measure` census** — **bound here**. Same shape, same file, and wrong today.
3. **"all sixty-seven resolve to `var(--fs-sentence)`"** — **bound here as the declared-tier
   property**, with the population figure moved out of prose and into the test that derives it. The
   *resolved* census — what an element renders at after the cascade — stays
   [DRC-4596](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596)'s AC-3 and
   [DRC-4602](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4602)'s composed audit. One number, one owner: this
   issue owns which rules *declare* the tier, they own what *resolves* to it.
4. **"`--fs-label` at 11px"** — **split**. The `:root` floor over every token is
   [DRC-4596](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4596)'s AC-1 and is not re-bound here. Bound here
   is the residue nothing else covers: the token's own value, the three sites that write 11px as a
   literal instead of the token, and the eight `--fs-*` tokens that have no caller at all — the
   property that keeps "collapsed into one tier" true rather than the property that a token is not
   retuned downward.
5. **The membership test** — **handed to [DRC-4602](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4602)**,
   unchanged from the filed reasoning. Its AC-1 and AC-2 build exactly the composed-cascade audit
   that a membership test enumerating elements would be, and a second implementation here would be
   the same number with two owners.
6. **The bound table** — **bound here**, as a derived capacity register over `web/*.js`. The
   helper-call half already re-derives exactly; the file-bucket half does not, and F9 named three
   files it puts on the wrong side.
