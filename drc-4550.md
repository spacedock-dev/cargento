[DRC-4550](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4550/under-no-annotations-reading-check-and-annotate-are-absent-from) — Under --no-annotations, reading_check and annotate are absent from /api/data rather than published

## User value

A page reading the payload can tell a build that predates the reading control from a run with annotations switched off. P2, sharpen: **an absent field is published at its absent value with the reason, as every other row field is.**

## Problem

`annotations.py:66-69` states that `reading_check` is published so the client page can tell "build predates the reading" from "check not run". Under `--no-annotations`, `reading_check` and `annotate` are omitted entirely from `/api/data` (`collection.update` adds an empty dict when `not config.annotations_enabled`). The client page expects `annotate` and `reading_check` to communicate their absent states, rather than omitting the keys entirely.

## Proposed approach

1. In `cargento/skills/cargento/cargento_runtime/aggregate.py`:
   - In `Application.collect`, when `not config.annotations_enabled`, publish:
     - `"annotate": False`
     - `"reading_check": annotation_store.ABSTENTION_CHECK_NOT_RUN` (`"not-run"`)
2. In `cargento/skills/cargento/tests/test_annotations.py`:
   - Update `test_the_capability_is_published_only_when_the_store_is_live` to assert `self.assertFalse(off_collect.get("annotate"))` and `self.assertEqual("not-run", off_collect.get("reading_check"))`.
3. Add dedicated unit tests proving `/api/data` carries `annotate: False` and `reading_check: "not-run"` when annotations are disabled.
4. Verify all frontend and backend contracts pass.
