[DRC-4348](https://linear.app/recce/issue/DRC-4348) — The published subagent roster has no cap: one lead row carried 294 elements and 30 KB, with no slice in the collector or the detail panel

## User value

A person running a busy lead agent for hours or days notices that the session payload does not bloat with hundreds of finished workers and browser rendering remains responsive. P2, move:none (bounds/scalability guard): without a roster cap, a single session card carries unbounded child elements and kilobytes of redundant historical data.

## Problem

Filed from review of the teammate-visibility fix (DRC-4344), which widened the published roster from live children of the last ninety seconds to every classified child and grandchild inside the display window. On a lead row that ran many tasks/workers, this produced 294 elements and ~30 KB on a single lead row, with no cap in the collector and no slice in the detail panel.

## Proposed approach

1. In `cargento/skills/cargento/cargento_runtime/sessions.py`:
   - Define `SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP: Final = 60` beside other published scalar bounds.
   - Declare `"subagents_omitted": 0` in `base_session()` so every session row carries the key.
2. In `cargento/skills/cargento/cargento_runtime/collectors/claude.py`:
   - Sort published roster: live workers first (`r.get("active") is True`), then finished workers ordered by newest finished first (`r.get("started_at") or 0` descending).
   - If `len(roster) > runtime_sessions.SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP`:
     Publish `roster[:runtime_sessions.SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP]` and record `subagents_omitted = len(roster) - runtime_sessions.SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP`.
   - Else publish `roster` and `subagents_omitted = 0`.
3. In `cargento/skills/cargento/cargento_runtime/web/next-session.js`:
   - In `nextSessionSubagents(session)`: when `session.subagents_omitted > 0`, append an honest omission notice below the subagent rows: `<div class="next-session-subagents-omitted">+${session.subagents_omitted} older finished worker${session.subagents_omitted === 1 ? "" : "s"} omitted</div>`.
4. In `cargento/skills/cargento/cargento_runtime/web/styles.css`:
   - Add `.next-session-subagents-omitted` styling matching muted secondary text.
5. In `docs/design-session-identity.md`:
   - Document `SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP` (60) and `subagents_omitted` beside existing published bounds.
6. In tests:
   - Update `DECLARED_SESSION_FIELDS` in `tests/test_sessions.py`.
   - Add tests in `tests/test_claude.py` verifying roster cap, ordering (active first, newest finished next), and omitted count.
   - Add tests in `tests/test_next_session.py` verifying detail panel omission notice.
   - Update asset byte and digest pins across `test_next_page.py`, `test_next_flag.py`, `test_focus.py`.

## Expected surface and tolerance

Estimate: ~45 net LOC across 7 files (`sessions.py`, `claude.py`, `next-session.js`, `styles.css`, `design-session-identity.md`, `test_sessions.py`, `test_claude.py`), tolerance ±25%.

## Acceptance criteria

**AC-1 — Published roster capped at SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP** (offline)
Verified by: A session with >60 subagents publishes exactly 60 elements in `subagents` and reports the excess in `subagents_omitted`.

**AC-2 — Ordering under cap preserves live workers and newest finished** (offline)
Verified by: Any active subagent is retained ahead of inactive subagents; inactive subagents are retained newest-first by `started_at`.

**AC-3 — Detail panel displays omitted count** (offline)
Verified by: `nextSessionSubagents` outputs `+N older finished worker(s) omitted` when `subagents_omitted > 0`.

**AC-4 — Every published row declares subagents_omitted** (offline)
Verified by: `PublishedSessionFieldSetTest` passes with `"subagents_omitted"` in `DECLARED_SESSION_FIELDS`.

**AC-5 — Bound documented in design doc** (offline)
Verified by: `docs/design-session-identity.md` documents `SUBAGENTS_ROSTER_CAP` and `subagents_omitted`.
