Sources for Cargento's embedded next-UI fonts

The design requests Space Grotesk weights 400, 500, 600, and 700 and
IBM Plex Mono weights 400, 500, and 600 upright plus 400 italic. The files
below are the Latin, Latin Extended, and Vietnamese WOFF2 subsets returned by
the Google Fonts CSS v2 endpoint. IBM Plex Mono came from:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=IBM+Plex+Mono:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;1,400&display=swap

The Space Grotesk subsets are unchanged. They came from the earlier combined
request, which also carried the retired Space Mono family:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700&display=swap
