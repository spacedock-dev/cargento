Sources for Cargento's embedded next-UI fonts

The design requests Space Grotesk weights 400, 500, 600, and 700 and
Space Mono weights 400 and 700. The files below are the Latin, Latin Extended,
and Vietnamese WOFF2 subsets returned by the Google Fonts CSS v2 endpoint:
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Space+Mono:wght@400;700&display=swap
